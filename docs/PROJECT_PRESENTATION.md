## General Introduction

### Current Situation

Bioengineering laboratories around the world conduct large-scale seedling propagation experiments for agricultural research and commercial production. These facilities:

- Cultivate thousands of seedlings across multiple crop species
- Conduct experiments to optimize propagation techniques
- Supply seedlings to investors, farms, and agricultural businesses
- Track complex relationships between research data and business deliverables

### Current Challenges

Most bioengineering labs operate without systematic digital tools, relying on:

- **Manual spreadsheets** for tracking experiments
- **Paper-based records** for seedling counts
- **Estimations** rather than data-driven production forecasts
- **Disconnected systems** between research and business operations

This leads to:

- ❌ Inaccurate delivery timelines to clients
- ❌ Lost research data and insights
- ❌ Inability to scale production confidently
- ❌ Poor inventory management of chemicals and equipment
- ❌ No systematic way to learn from past experiments

---

## Problem Statement

1. **No systematic seedling growth tracking** — Week-over-week experimental data is lost or scattered across spreadsheets with no centralized monitoring

2. **Inaccurate production capacity calculations** — Unable to predict delivery timelines based on historical experimental data and survival rates

3. **Missed business opportunities** — Cannot confidently commit to client contracts without data-driven production forecasts

4. **Inventory management chaos** — Chemical expiry, equipment maintenance, and plant batch tracking done manually leading to waste and safety risks

5. **Disconnected research and business operations** — Experimental insights don't inform business decisions; no feedback loop between production and research

---

## Project Vision

1. **Centralized inventory management** — Real-time tracking of plant species, batches, chemicals, and equipment with automated alerts and full audit trails

2. **Data-driven production forecasting** — Seedling Production Calculator that uses historical experimental data to generate accurate delivery timelines and resource requirements

3. **Integrated research tracking** — Digital experiment management with weekly growth data collection, protocol libraries, and statistical analysis

4. **Professional business operations** — Contract generation, client relationship management, and revenue tracking powered by actual production data

5. **Continuous improvement feedback loop** — Experimental insights inform business decisions; contract outcomes validate research data; creating smarter forecasts over time

## Project Scopes

### 1. Inventory Management Module

**Plant Species & stock Management**

- CRUD operations for plant species catalog
- Plant stock tracking linked to species
- Growth stage monitoring and health scoring
- Image upload and visual documentation
- Search, filter, and sort capabilities

**Chemical Inventory**

- Chemical database with hazard classification (GHS)
- Expiry date tracking and automated alerts
- Safety Data Sheet (SDS) links
- Usage records and consumption tracking
- Low stock notifications

**Equipment Management**

- Equipment catalog with specifications
- Maintenance scheduling and history
- Usage logs and booking system
- Depreciation tracking
- Status monitoring (available, in-use, maintenance)

**Dashboard & Analytics**

- Real-time KPI widgets (inventory levels, active batches, expiring chemicals)
- Transaction history feed
- Trend analysis charts
- Inventory valuation reports
